## Full note (shown when the short note is tapped, and on the You tab, under About)

> **About visa and entry rules**
>
> Travel App suggests what to check before a trip, based on your passport country and where you are going. The
> suggestions come from rules we write and review by hand for European destinations. They are **not** legal or
> official advice.
>
> - Rules for visas, passports, ID cards, travel authorisations (for example ETIAS), health documents and customs
>   change often, sometimes at short notice.
> - What applies to you can depend on details the app does not know: residence permits, a second nationality, the
>   reason for your trip, how long you stay, or where you travel next.
> - **Always check** with the official websites of the country you are going to (government or embassy), your own
>   government's travel advice and your airline, before you book and again before you leave.
>
> We are not responsible if you are refused boarding or entry because you relied only on the app. If you see a
> hint that looks wrong, please tell us at **[CONTACT EMAIL]**.
